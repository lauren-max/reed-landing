How it works

Reading builds vocabulary.
Reed makes it visible.

01
Save a word

When you encounter an unfamiliar word, open Reed and save it along with the book it came from, its definition, and an example sentence.

02
Build your glossary

Every word you save becomes part of your personal glossary, a living record of your reading and the language you have made your own.

03
Share with readers

Follow friends and see the words they are saving. Like and comment on their finds. Discover new books through the vocabulary people are pulling from them.

Features

Everything a reader needs.

Word saving with context

Save any word with its definition, an example sentence, and the book it came from.

Personal glossary

Your saved words accumulate into a searchable, browsable glossary that grows with your reading life.

Social reading feed

Follow friends and see what they are reading and learning. A quiet, thoughtful feed with no algorithmic noise.

Book discovery

Tap any book to preview it or purchase it directly through Bookshop.org, supporting independent booksellers.

Sign in your way

Sign in with Apple, Google, or email, whichever you prefer.

Classroom tools

Assign word lists, track student progress, and bring vocabulary instruction into the reading students are already doing.

For schools

Built for the classroom as much as the armchair.

Reed is designed to work inside schools and universities as a vocabulary and reading comprehension tool. It meets students where they already are, in the middle of a book, and makes the language they encounter there something worth keeping.

Teachers get a clear view of what their students are reading and learning, with tools to guide and assess vocabulary growth over time.

Assign word lists

Create and assign curated vocabulary lists tied to class texts, ensuring every student engages with the language that matters for your curriculum.

Monitor student progress

See which words each student has saved, how their glossary is growing, and where they may need more support.

Connect reading to vocabulary

Words are always saved alongside the books they came from, keeping vocabulary instruction rooted in real reading.

Works at every level

From middle school to university, Reed scales to meet students wherever they are in their reading lives.
